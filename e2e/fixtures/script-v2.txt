ACT ONE, SCENE 5 - THE TRAIN PLATFORM

(Night. Steam rolls across the platform. A porter pushes a cart of instrument cases.)

JOE
Keep your head down and your case up. Nobody looks twice at a bass fiddle.

JERRY
Nobody looks twice at a bass fiddle wearing heels either, I hope.

(A whistle. The band girls hurry past in a flurry of hat boxes.)

(A conductor checks his pocket watch, then checks it again.)

SUE
Ladies, on the train! Sweet Sue needs a saxophone and a bass player, and she needs them tonight.
13

JOE
Then Sweet Sue is in luck, ma'am. Josephine, tenor sax.

JERRY
Daphne. Bass. Classically trained.

SUE
Whatever you are, you're hired. Bienstock, the tickets!

(The train doors slide open. Light spills onto the platform.)

ACT ONE, SCENE 6 - THE SLEEPER CAR

(The car sways. Curtains on every berth. A ukulele plays somewhere.)

SUGAR
Anybody got a corkscrew? It's a long way to Florida.

JERRY
I have a corkscrew in my other purse.

(Lights shift. The berths glow like paper lanterns.)
